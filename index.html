Referee strategy

Academic referee

A university teacher who can verify academic potential, the graduation project, collaboration and readiness for master’s study.

Professional referee

A non-relative supervisor, senior stakeholder or client who directly observed leadership, delivery and relationship-building.

Chevening initially requires referee contact details. If shortlisted, two English reference letters must be uploaded at least seven working days before the interview. A relative or friend must not be used as a referee.
