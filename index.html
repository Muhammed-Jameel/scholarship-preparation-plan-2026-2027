Track 4 — Spain-linked Erasmus Mundus

Two fully funded joint master’s routes match the target field and include a Spanish consortium university. These are European mobility programmes rather than degrees studied only in Spain.

Expected Nov 2026–Jan 2027
Date status: the 2027 scholarship calls are not yet published. Planning dates below are based on the official 2026-entry cycles: EMAI accepted scholarship applications from 15 November to 20 December, and EDISS from 3 November to 8 January. Replace these dates immediately when the 2027 calls open.

Best-fit programmes and full funding

Priority: EDISS

Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in Engineering of Data-intensive Intelligent Software Systems. The consortium includes the University of the Balearic Islands in Spain; Spain is a possible second-year specialisation destination under programme mobility rules. The EMJM award waives the €9,000 annual participation cost, includes insurance and pays €1,400 monthly for 24 months. It fits Mohammed’s software-engineering, deployment and intelligent-system experience most directly.

AI reach: EMAI

Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in Artificial Intelligence. Coordinated by Pompeu Fabra University in Barcelona, with mobility across the consortium. The scholarship pays €1,400 monthly for 24 months and covers tuition, travel, installation, visa, insurance and other mandatory participation costs. Selection gives substantial weight to grades and academic excellence, so this is an ambitious application with a 65.68% final result.

Track 5 — Switzerland: ETH Zurich ESOP

The strongest verified full-cost taught-master’s route in Switzerland that matches the target field. Keep it as a controlled reach, not as a replacement for the more realistic applications.

Official: 1–30 Nov 2026
Reality check: ESOP expects a bachelor result in roughly the top 10% (grade A). A 65.68% result is not improved or replaced by IELTS, GRE or work experience. Apply only if the academic record, class-position evidence and referees can honestly support exceptional standing and the ETH admission profile is satisfied.

Funding and proposed programme

ETH MSc Computer Science

A two-year, English-taught, 120-ECTS programme with specialisations including Machine Intelligence and Secure and Reliable Systems. The application requires C1 English, two English recommendation letters—preferably academic—and a programme-specific motivation letter.

ESOP coverage

CHF 12,000 per semester for living and study expenses plus a tuition-fee waiver for the regular three- or four-semester duration. Approximately 60 awards are planned for 2027/28, with decisions by the end of March. The master’s admission and ESOP request are submitted together through ETH eApply.

August–15 October 2026: make a go/no-go decision

15 October–30 November 2026: prepare and submit if the gate is passed

Do not use the Swiss Government Excellence Scholarship for this master’s plan

The Swiss Embassy’s Iraq guidance explicitly states that the federal research scholarships do not fund bachelor’s or master’s study. They are mainly for researchers who already hold a master’s degree, while first-master’s scholarships are limited to eligible arts applicants. This route should be reconsidered only later for doctoral or postgraduate research.
